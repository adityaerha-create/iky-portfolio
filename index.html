AR FLOS

Augmented Reality App for
Flower Anatomy Education

“

AR FLOS was designed to make botanical education interactive and accessible. The main UX challenge was seamlessly integrating the camera-based Augmented Reality view with the educational UI panels, ensuring that the flower information is easily readable without obstructing the 3D model viewing experience.

”

KADA KART

Indonesian Racing Game
Inspired by Kadaplak from Bandung

“

Developed during the IGDX Bootcamp, Kada Kart aimed to bring local Indonesian flavors into the fast-paced kart racing genre. My focus was on creating a vibrant, energetic UI that communicates racing telemetry—like speed, lap times, and items—clearly, while maintaining the fun, culturally rich identity of the game.

”

PINDAHAN

Multiplayer Game
Inspiredby Moving Out Game

“

As an academic project at MMTC Yogyakarta, Pindahan was a deep dive into multiplayer UI design. Inspired by 'Moving Out', the interface needed to be chaotic yet highly legible.
